# How Magnets Work

## Script

How do magnets work without even touching each other. Hawakan mo ang dalawang magnet at ilapit, mararamdaman mo ang isang invisible force na humihila o tumutulak sa kamay mo. Parang magic, pero it is all governed by the quantum motion of tiny subatomic particles.

Lahat ng bagay sa paligid natin ay gawa sa atoms. Sa gitna ay ang nucleus, at pinapalibutan ito ng electrons. These electrons do not just orbit. They also spin on their own axis, tulad ng umiikot na trumpo.

In physics, any moving electric charge generates a magnetic field. Dahil may negative charge ang electron at patuloy itong umiikot, every single electron acts like a microscopic permanent magnet na may sariling North at South pole.

Kung lahat ng atoms ay may electrons, bakit hindi magnetic ang kahoy o plastic. Sa karamihan ng materyales, electrons come in pairs with opposite spins. One spins up and one spins down, completely canceling their magnetic fields to zero.

Pero sa mga ferromagnetic materials tulad ng iron, nickel, at cobalt, iba ang sitwasyon. Marami silang unpaired electrons sa outer shells. Dahil walang katapat na magka-cancel, their tiny magnetic moments remain active and reinforce one another.

Kahit sa isang piraso ng bakal, hindi ito agad dumidikit sa ref. Inside raw iron, billions of atoms group into small pockets called magnetic domains. Sa natural state nito, these domains point in totally random directions, canceling each other out.

Kapag inilapit mo ang isang magnet, something dramatic happens. The external magnetic force pushes against the boundaries, causing all the scrambled domains to swing and lock into the exact same direction. Ang dating ordinaryong bakal, nagiging magnet na rin.

Kapag nakapila na ang lahat ng domains, their individual forces combine into one continuous magnetic field. Invisible flux lines erupt from the North pole, curve smoothly through three-dimensional space, and loop back into the South pole.

Dito nagmumula ang attraction at repulsion. Kapag pinaglapit mo ang North at South, their magnetic lines merge naturally into a straight bridge, pulling them together. Pero kapag pinagtabi mo ang two identical poles, their field lines collide and push outward.

May dalawang klase ng magnet sa technology. Permanent magnets, tulad ng neodymium, keep their atomic domains locked permanently. Samantala, electromagnets use electric current in copper coils, allowing engineers to turn giant magnetic fields on and off in an instant.

Mula sa credit cards hanggang sa high-speed maglev trains, it all traces back to microscopic electron spins working together. Ikaw, ano ang pinaka-astig na gamit ng magnet na nasubukan mo. I-comment mo sa baba. Follow me for more tech explained. See yah.

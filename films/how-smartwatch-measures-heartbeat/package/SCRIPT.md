# SCRIPT: How Your Watch Measures Your Heartbeat with Light
**Profile:** Tech Explanasyon (TE)  
**Speaker:** Carla  
**Target Duration:** ~100 - 115s  
**Status:** Approved by Alvin  

---

### SCENE 1: Hook & Look Underneath Your Watch (0:00 - 0:14)
**Audio:**
"Paano nalalaman ng smartwatch ang tibok ng puso mo, gamit lang ang ilaw? Look underneath your watch. Those green lights are part of a system called photoplethysmography, or PPG. Mahabang pangalan, pero simple ang idea: your pulse changes how much light comes back from your skin."

---

### SCENE 2: The Emitter and the Photodiode (0:14 - 0:26)
**Audio:**
"The watch shines light into your wrist. Some gets absorbed, and some scatters back toward a light detector called a photodiode. It measures light intensity, not a picture of your veins."

---

### SCENE 3: The Heartbeat Pressure Wave (0:26 - 0:38)
**Audio:**
"With each heartbeat, a pressure wave travels through your arteries. Blood volume in the small vessels under your skin rises and falls. Because blood absorbs green light, those changes affect the amount returning to the sensor."

---

### SCENE 4: The Absorption Cycle (0:38 - 0:50)
**Audio:**
"More blood generally means more green light absorbed, so less returns. Between pulses, the pattern reverses. Tiny changes, repeated over time, create a wave the watch can analyze."

---

### SCENE 5: Strobe Sampling (0:50 - 1:02)
**Audio:**
"Hindi ibig sabihin na bawat flash ng ilaw ay isang heartbeat. The LEDs can flash much faster than your pulse. The watch takes many samples, and software looks for the repeating pulse pattern."

---

### SCENE 6: Pulse Frequency & BPM Math (1:02 - 1:14)
**Audio:**
"If that pattern repeats once every second, that corresponds to sixty beats per minute. Twice every second would be one hundred twenty. These are examples, not target numbers."

---

### SCENE 7: Real-World Challenges: Fit, Motion, & Cold (1:14 - 1:28)
**Audio:**
"Pero may challenge. Moving your wrist or letting the watch slide can also change the light signal. Cold skin can reduce blood flow near the surface, making the pulse harder to detect. That is why fit, movement, and the algorithm affect the estimate."

---

### SCENE 8: Optical PPG vs Electrical ECG (1:28 - 1:38)
**Audio:**
"And this optical pulse signal is different from an ECG, which measures electrical activity. Different sensors, different information."

---

### SCENE 9: Call to Action & Closing Gate (1:38 - 1:48)
**Audio:**
"Ikaw, napansin mo na bang nagbabago ang reading kapag maluwag ang relo mo? Follow me for more tech explained. See yah."
